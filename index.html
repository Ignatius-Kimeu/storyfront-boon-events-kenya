The idea

Small events shouldn’t need a big budget or a project manager

Mercy started Boon after struggling to put together her own daughter’s first birthday and two baby showers for close friends. Chasing a venue, a caterer, a tent company and a decorator separately is where the cost and the stress come from.

So Boon does the opposite. One booking covers the garden, the tent, the seating, the styling and the buffet — at a per-head rate you can work out on your fingers. You bring your colours, your cake, your music and your photographer.
