About

Boon started with one very stressful first birthday

Mercy runs Boon Events Kenya out of Karen, Nairobi. Here’s how it began, in her own words.

In Mercy’s words

“I turned a passion into a business”

“I began this business after struggling to plan my daughter’s 1st birthday and two of my besties’ baby showers. I have always enjoyed styling and decor, and I’ve been blessed to turn my passion into a business.”

“My main goal has been to ease the burden of small event planning, by availing all necessary services under one package.”

“Currently I’m breaking the societal norm of overpricing couples who wish to enjoy their wedding without incurring debt — hence the introduction of the wedding reception package at a similar rate. Looking forward to serving you.”

Mercy

Founder, Boon Events Kenya

How Boon works

Everything under one package

The whole model exists to remove the four separate suppliers most small events end up juggling.

The venue is hers

Boon only uses its own three venues in Karen. No garden fee, no third-party hire, no negotiating with a venue manager you’ve never met.

The decor is hers

Tenting, chiavari seating, tie backs, dressed tables, charger plates, runners, napkins and centrepieces — all in house, all in the rate.

The kitchen is hers

A full buffet with water and sodas, cooked for your numbers. It’s the part clients message about most often afterwards.
