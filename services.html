Before you book

Kindly note

Mercy publishes these terms up front so there are no surprises on the day. They’re reproduced here exactly as she words them.

  • Minimum booking is 10 adults on weekends, 15 on weekdays.
  • Decor excludes backdrops, balloons, signages, banners & kiddie setups.
  • Clients choose their own theme colours.
  • Cake is allowed, and there is no cakeage charge.
  • Clients source their own music and photography.
  • No food may be brought in from outside.
  • The alcoholic drinks policy differs with each venue.
  • Damage to linens caused by candles, painting etc. is replaced by the client at full price.
  • Deposits are forfeited for cancellations or postponements made within 48 hours of the event date. Deposits are non-refundable.

Questions

The things people ask first

Where are you based?

Mercy is based in Karen, Nairobi — and so are the three venues you get to choose from.

Do you travel to my venue?

No. Boon doesn’t do house calls or venue calls, and only uses its own venues in Karen. That is exactly why the rate stays where it is.

Can I change my event date?

Yes, as long as you do so more than 48 hours before the booked date, so you don’t lose your deposit.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes. The amount depends on the number of people you expect to host.

When is the final payment due?

Immediately the setup is completed on the day of your event.

How soon do I need to pay the booking deposit?

Booking is on a first-pay basis. It’s entirely up to you when you’d like to make your payment — but the date goes to whoever pays first.

Can you take a last-minute booking?

Yes — as long as the venue is free, Mercy can book you a day before the event. That needs at least 24 hours’ notice.
